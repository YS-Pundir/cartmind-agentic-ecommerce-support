# 1. Customer Support Policy Overview

Nimbus Commerce operates an online marketplace selling apparel,
footwear, electronics, home appliances, beauty and personal-care
products, furniture, sports equipment, accessories, and selected digital
products.

This knowledge base defines the default customer-support rules for
orders placed directly through Nimbus Commerce. Individual product
pages, promotional campaigns, seller-specific terms, and legally
mandatory consumer rights may override a general rule when explicitly
stated.

Customer-support agents should identify the customer's **order number,
product category, fulfillment method, payment method, order status,
delivery date, and issue type** before deciding eligibility. If a policy
depends on information stored in the order system, the agent must use
the order-data tool rather than guessing.

A policy answer should distinguish between: - what the customer is
eligible to request, - what Nimbus Commerce has approved, - what action
has already been performed, - and what action is still pending.

When two rules appear to conflict, the more specific rule normally takes
precedence over a general rule. For example, a category-specific
warranty term overrides the generic warranty term, and a
promotion-specific return rule overrides the normal return window when
the promotion explicitly states that restriction.

## 2.2 Clothing and Footwear

Clothing and footwear may be returned within 30 calendar days of
delivery. Items should be unworn, unwashed, free of odors, stains, pet
hair, or other signs of use, and should retain original tags when tags
were supplied.

Shoes should be tried indoors on a clean surface. Excessive wear to
soles may make the item ineligible for a standard change-of-mind return,
although manufacturing defects remain subject to the applicable defect
and warranty process.

## 2.3 Electronics

Electronics have a 14-day standard return window. The product must
include required accessories, manuals, adapters, and other supplied
components. Nimbus Commerce may verify serial numbers and may require a
device reset or diagnostic inspection before accepting a refund.

A device that becomes defective after the standard return period is
normally handled through the applicable warranty process rather than the
standard change-of-mind return process.

## 2.4 Beauty and Personal Care

Unopened beauty products may normally be returned within 30 days.
Products whose protective seal has been broken may be excluded for
hygiene reasons unless the claim concerns a defect, contamination,
incorrect item, or fulfillment error.

Customers should not send leaking, contaminated, or unsafe products
through ordinary return channels without first contacting support.

## 2.5 Furniture and Large Items

Furniture returns normally require a scheduled reverse pickup because of
size and handling requirements. The customer must not disassemble or
alter a product in a way that prevents safe inspection unless the return
instructions explicitly permit disassembly.

Assembly damage caused by the customer is not treated as a normal return
reason. Manufacturing defects, transit damage, or incorrect items follow
the damaged-item or wrong-item process.

# 3. Return Conditions and Refund Eligibility

A standard return must satisfy the applicable category window and
condition requirements. Nimbus Commerce may reject a return if the
product is materially used, intentionally damaged, incomplete,
counterfeit, substituted, or returned with missing essential components.

A return does not automatically mean that a refund has already been
issued. The normal sequence is: return request, eligibility validation,
return authorization, physical receipt, inspection, approval, refund
initiation, payment-provider processing, and customer account
confirmation.

If an item fails inspection because of customer-caused damage, Nimbus
Commerce may offer a partial refund, store credit, replacement option,
or return the item to the customer. The final action depends on the
severity of the issue and the applicable category rules.

For seller-fulfilled products, the seller's approved return conditions
may apply when explicitly identified on the order.

# 4. COD Refund Timelines

Cash on Delivery (COD) orders require a separate refund workflow because
the original payment was collected in cash or through a delivery-agent
collection process rather than through an online payment authorization.

## 4.1 Standard COD Refund Timeline

For an approved COD return, Nimbus Commerce normally initiates the
refund within **3 business days after return inspection is completed**.
The customer may choose an eligible bank transfer destination or Nimbus
Commerce store credit, subject to the options displayed for the order.

After Nimbus Commerce initiates a bank refund, the customer's bank may
require an additional **2--5 business days** to reflect the amount.
Nimbus Commerce's support agent should distinguish between the internal
refund-initiation timestamp and the bank-posting timestamp.

## 4.2 COD Refund Exceptions

If the customer has not provided valid bank details, the refund remains
pending until valid details are received. A refund can also be paused if
the order is under a fraud review, the returned item does not match the
order, or required return components are missing.

If a COD refund has been marked "processed" but has not appeared after
the normal banking period, support should first verify the transaction
reference and destination details before escalating to the payments
team.

## 4.3 COD Partial Refunds

For orders containing multiple products, an approved return of only some
products produces a partial refund. Discounts, bundle pricing, shipping
fees, loyalty-point usage, and promotional credits may affect the
refundable amount.

Example: if a customer buys three items under a bundle discount and
returns one item, the refund is not necessarily the standalone list
price. The refund engine recalculates the allocation of discounts
according to the order's promotion rules.

# 5. Delivery SLAs

Delivery Service Level Agreements (SLAs) define expected delivery ranges
after an order is confirmed and released for fulfillment. An SLA is an
operational target and does not mean that every parcel is guaranteed to
arrive at the exact end of the stated range.

## 5.1 Domestic Delivery SLA

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Service         Processing target    Delivery target      Typical total
  -------------- ------------------ ------------------ ------------------
  Standard       0--2 business days 2--5 business days 2--7 business days

  Express         0--1 business day 1--2 business days 1--3 business days

  Priority metro Same/next business     1 business day 1--2 business days
                                day                    

  Large-item     1--3 business days 3--7 business days     4--10 business
  delivery                                                           days
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

Business days exclude weekends and configured public holidays. Weather
events, carrier disruptions, customs processing, remote-area
restrictions, and customer-requested delivery changes can extend the
operational timeline.

## 5.2 Delivery Status Definitions

**Order confirmed** means payment and order validation have succeeded.

**Processing** means the order is being prepared but has not necessarily
been handed to the carrier.

**Shipped/in transit** means the parcel has entered the carrier network.

**Out for delivery** means the carrier has assigned the parcel to a
delivery route.

**Delivered** means the carrier reports a successful delivery event.

**Delivery exception** means a carrier or operational issue is
preventing normal progress.

## 5.3 Late Delivery

A customer should normally be treated as experiencing a late delivery
when the current date exceeds the applicable promised delivery range
without an approved exception.

For standard domestic parcels, support should first inspect tracking
data. If there has been no carrier scan for 48 hours while the parcel is
in transit, the support agent may open a carrier investigation.

A delayed parcel should not automatically be declared lost. The
lost-package workflow requires the applicable investigation threshold to
be reached.

# 6. Reverse-Pickup Eligibility

Reverse pickup is the process by which Nimbus Commerce or its logistics
partner collects an approved return from the customer's address.

## 6.1 Eligibility

Reverse pickup is normally available for: - domestic clothing and
footwear returns, - electronics eligible for return, - appliances, -
furniture, - qualifying seller-fulfilled items, - damaged-item claims, -
incorrect-item claims.

Availability depends on the customer's postal area, carrier coverage,
parcel dimensions, product category, and reason for return.

## 6.2 Pickup Requirements

The customer must package the item according to the return instructions.
The product should contain required accessories and documentation. For
large items, the customer may be required to make the product accessible
to the pickup team.

A customer should not hand over a different item because it has a
similar appearance. Serial-numbered products are checked against the
original order.

## 6.3 Failed Pickup

If a pickup fails because the carrier did not arrive, the customer was
unavailable, or packaging was not ready, support should record the
reason. A carrier-attributable failed pickup can normally be rescheduled
without an additional charge.

Repeated customer-attributable failures may result in a different return
method, such as a drop-off label.

## 6.4 Remote Areas

Reverse pickup may be unavailable in remote or carrier-restricted areas.
In such cases Nimbus Commerce may provide a drop-off label, arrange an
alternative carrier, or perform a manual support review.

window. A product can be outside its return window and still be eligible
for warranty service if the defect falls within the applicable warranty
term.

applicable, description of the defect, and requested evidence. Support
may provide troubleshooting instructions before approving a replacement
or repair.

If remote troubleshooting cannot resolve the issue, the case may be
routed to the warranty team or manufacturer service center.

# 8. Order-Cancellation Policy

Customers can request cancellation before an order reaches a
non-cancellable fulfillment stage.

## 8.1 Cancellation by Status

  Order status       Cancellation
  ------------------ --------------------------------------------------
  Payment pending    Normally allowed
  Confirmed          Usually allowed if fulfillment has not started
  Processing         Case-dependent
  Packed             Usually not cancellable
  Shipped            Not cancellable; customer may use return process
  Out for delivery   Not cancellable
  Delivered          Return process applies

Cancellation is a request, not a guarantee. If the warehouse has already
packed or transferred the parcel to a carrier, cancellation may no
longer be operationally possible.

## 8.2 Cancellation Refund

If cancellation is approved before shipment, Nimbus Commerce initiates
the refund according to the payment method. Online payment refunds
generally follow the payment provider's processing time.

COD orders that are cancelled before dispatch do not require a COD
refund because no cash payment was collected.

## 8.3 Customer Refuses Delivery

Refusing delivery is not the preferred cancellation method. The customer
should request cancellation before shipment where possible. A refused
parcel may incur operational handling costs and can delay the refund.

# 9. Loyalty-Points Redemption Policy

Nimbus Rewards points can be earned on qualifying purchases and redeemed
for eligible discounts or benefits.

## 9.1 Redemption Rules

-   Minimum redemption: 500 points.
-   Standard conversion: 100 points = €1 discount.
-   Maximum points redemption: 30% of eligible merchandise subtotal.
-   Points cannot normally be exchanged for cash.
-   Points cannot be transferred between customer accounts.
-   Points cannot be used to pay taxes or certain regulated fees.
-   Some promotional items may be excluded.

The redemption interface displays the maximum number of points that can
be applied to a particular order.

## 9.2 Points Expiration

Unless a campaign states otherwise, reward points expire **12 months
after the month in which they were earned**. A customer may see pending
points before they become redeemable.

Cancelled or returned purchases may reverse points that were originally
awarded for those purchases.

## 9.3 Refunds and Loyalty Points

When an order paid partly with points is refunded, the cash portion is
refunded according to the original payment method and eligible points
are normally returned to the customer's rewards balance.

If points were earned from the purchase being returned, those earned
points may be removed.

## 9.4 Promotional Multipliers

During a 2x or 3x points campaign, the campaign-specific terms determine
which products qualify. Promotional points may have a separate
expiration date if explicitly stated.

# 10. Payment-Failure and Retry Policy

Payment failures can occur because of insufficient funds, bank
restrictions, expired cards, authentication failures, provider outages,
incorrect billing information, or risk controls.

## 10.1 Retry Sequence

For eligible online card payments, Nimbus Commerce may allow: 1. the
original authorization attempt, 2. a customer-initiated retry, 3. a
second automated retry where supported, 4. a payment-method change, 5.
order cancellation if payment remains incomplete.

A failed payment does not necessarily mean that an order was created
successfully.

## 10.2 Temporary Authorization

Customers may see a temporary bank authorization even when an order
fails. Such an authorization may disappear automatically according to
the bank's release process. Support should not promise an exact bank
release time unless the payment provider has confirmed one.

## 10.3 Duplicate Charges

If a customer reports two charges, support must check the payment
records and order IDs. Two bank entries do not automatically mean two
completed charges; one may be a temporary authorization.

If two successful captures exist for one order without a corresponding
second order, the case must be escalated to Payments.

## 10.4 Payment Retry Limits

Repeated payment failures can trigger a security review. Customers
should not be advised to repeatedly retry a transaction indefinitely. If
multiple payment methods fail, the agent should recommend verifying
billing details and using another supported method.

# 11. Size-Exchange Policy

Size exchanges apply primarily to clothing, footwear, and selected
wearable products.

A size exchange may be requested within the category's return window.
The original item must normally be unworn, unwashed, and in resalable
condition.

## 11.1 Exchange Process

1.  Customer selects the size-exchange option.
2.  System checks eligibility.
3.  Requested replacement size is reserved if available.
4.  Return shipment is initiated.
5.  Returned product is inspected.
6.  Replacement is dispatched according to exchange rules.

If the requested size is unavailable, Nimbus Commerce may offer a
different size, store credit, or refund.

## 11.2 Price Difference

For an exchange within the same product and model, Nimbus Commerce
normally does not charge a price difference caused solely by a size
variation.

If the customer changes to a different model, color, or product, the
transaction may be processed as a return plus a new purchase.

## 11.3 Footwear

Shoes must be tried indoors and returned with the original box where
possible. Visible outdoor wear can make a standard size exchange
ineligible.

# 12. Damaged-Item Claim Process

Customers who receive visibly damaged or defective products should
contact Nimbus Commerce promptly.

## 12.1 Claim Window

A transit-damage claim should normally be reported within **48 hours of
delivery**. Manufacturing-defect claims may follow the applicable
warranty or defect process even when the 48-hour transit-damage window
has passed.

## 12.2 Evidence

Depending on the claim, support may request: - photos of the product, -
photos of the shipping box, - photos of shipping labels, - close-up
images of the damage, - video showing a functional defect, - serial
number, - packaging condition.

Evidence requirements are proportional to the claim. Customers should
not be required to provide unnecessary information.

## 12.3 Resolution

Eligible claims may result in: - replacement, - repair, - full refund, -
partial refund, - store credit, - manufacturer warranty service.

If the product is unsafe, the customer should stop using it and follow
the support team's handling instructions.

## 12.4 Severe Damage

For broken glass, exposed electrical components, leaking batteries,
overheating devices, or other potentially hazardous conditions, the
standard return workflow should not be used until support has provided
safe handling instructions.

# 13. International Shipping Restrictions

Nimbus Commerce ships internationally to selected destinations.
Availability depends on product type, destination, customs requirements,
carrier capability, and applicable trade restrictions.

## 13.1 Restricted Categories

The following categories may be restricted internationally: -
lithium-battery products, - aerosols, - flammable materials, -
chemicals, - pressurized containers, - certain cosmetics, - perishable
products, - oversized furniture, - products requiring professional
installation, - products restricted by destination-country law.

A product may be available for domestic purchase but unavailable for a
particular international destination.

## 13.2 Customs and Import Charges

International customers may be responsible for customs duties, import
taxes, brokerage fees, or other destination-country charges when these
are not explicitly included at checkout.

Customs authorities may inspect shipments and may delay delivery. Nimbus
Commerce cannot guarantee the exact customs processing time.

## 13.3 International Returns

International return eligibility depends on product category and
destination. Customers may be required to use a designated carrier or
return method.

If the return is caused by a Nimbus Commerce fulfillment error, Nimbus
Commerce may cover reasonable return costs subject to investigation.
Change-of-mind international returns may require the customer to pay
return shipping.

## 13.4 Prohibited Destinations

Nimbus Commerce does not ship to embargoed or operationally unsupported
destinations. The checkout system is the final authority for whether a
specific destination and product combination can be ordered.

# 15. Missing, Delayed, or Lost Package Policy

A package is considered potentially lost when carrier tracking has
remained inactive beyond the investigation threshold or the carrier
explicitly confirms loss.

For domestic shipments, support may initiate a carrier investigation
after 48 hours without movement when the parcel is already outside its
expected delivery range. For high-value shipments, the threshold may be
reduced when the tracking event indicates a serious exception.

A customer should not be asked to reorder immediately unless there is a
clear business reason. Depending on inventory and customer preference,
Nimbus Commerce may provide a replacement or refund after the
appropriate investigation stage.

If the carrier confirms delivery but the customer cannot locate the
parcel, support should verify delivery coordinates or proof-of-delivery
information where available. The customer may be asked to check common
delivery locations and household or building recipients before a claim
is opened.

# 16. Wrong Item and Incorrect Quantity Policy

If a customer receives a product different from the ordered product,
Nimbus Commerce treats the issue as a fulfillment error.

The customer should provide the order number and, where requested, a
photo of the received item and shipping label. Nimbus Commerce may
arrange a free reverse pickup and ship the correct product if available.

If the customer received fewer units than ordered, support should check
whether the order was intentionally split into multiple shipments. If no
second shipment exists, the missing quantity is treated as a fulfillment
discrepancy.

Customers should not be charged return shipping for confirmed Nimbus
Commerce fulfillment errors.

# 17. Partial Shipment and Split-Order Policy

An order containing multiple products may be shipped in separate parcels
because items can originate from different warehouses or sellers.

The order system and tracking page should show each shipment separately
when split fulfillment occurs. A customer should not assume that an item
is missing merely because the first parcel arrived without all products.

If one shipment is delivered and another remains in transit within its
SLA, support should provide the expected tracking information. If the
second shipment exceeds the applicable investigation threshold, support
may open a delivery investigation.

Refunds for individual missing products should not be issued before
checking whether a remaining shipment is active unless policy or
customer circumstances justify an exception.

# 18. Address Change Policy

Customers may request an address change while an order remains in an
editable fulfillment stage.

Once an order is packed or handed to the carrier, the address generally
cannot be changed directly. The customer may be offered carrier
redirection if available, or the order may proceed to delivery and then
use the applicable return process.

Address changes cannot be used to redirect controlled or restricted
products to an unsupported destination.

If a customer enters an incorrect address and the parcel is returned to
Nimbus Commerce, support should determine whether a reshipment is
possible and whether additional shipping charges apply.

# 19. Promotional Discount and Coupon Policy

Promotional codes are subject to their individual campaign terms.

Common restrictions include: - minimum basket value, - eligible product
categories, - one code per order, - customer-account restrictions, -
campaign expiration, - geographic restrictions, - seller restrictions, -
exclusion of already discounted items.

A coupon cannot normally be converted to cash.

## 19.1 Failed Coupon Application

If a coupon fails at checkout, the customer should first verify the
campaign requirements. Support may manually investigate only when the
customer provides enough information to reproduce the issue.

## 19.2 Returns After Promotion

When a customer returns one product from a discounted multi-item order,
the refund engine recalculates the applicable discount. A customer
should not automatically expect the full undiscounted price of the
returned product.

## 19.3 Price Adjustments

Nimbus Commerce may provide a price adjustment within 7 days when a
qualifying price difference is caused by a Nimbus Commerce price change.
Promotional coupon differences are excluded unless the promotion terms
state otherwise.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 20. Gift Card and Store Credit Policy

Nimbus Commerce gift cards may be used for eligible purchases and are
generally not redeemable for cash.

Gift cards cannot normally be transferred between accounts after
redemption. Customers who have accidentally purchased an unused gift
card should contact support promptly; cancellation may be considered
before redemption depending on the gift-card terms.

Store credit issued as a refund is linked to the customer's account and
cannot normally be withdrawn as cash.

If an order paid partly with gift-card balance is returned, the
gift-card portion is normally returned to the same gift-card or
store-credit balance, while any remaining payment-method portion follows
the applicable refund process.

Lost physical gift cards require proof of purchase and may be subject to
identity verification before replacement.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 21. Account, Privacy, and Security Support

Customer-support agents must not request passwords, full payment-card
numbers, one-time authentication codes, or other authentication secrets.

If a customer cannot access an account, support should use the approved
identity-verification workflow. Agents must not bypass security checks
solely because the customer knows an order number.

Suspicious account activity, unauthorized order claims, repeated failed
authentication attempts, or unusual payment activity should be routed to
the Risk/Security team.

Privacy-related requests, such as requests concerning personal data,
account records, or deletion, must be routed to the designated Privacy
team when the frontline agent cannot complete the approved workflow.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 22. Product-Specific Final-Sale Rules

Certain products are final sale because they are customized,
hygienically sensitive, perishable, or otherwise unsuitable for ordinary
resale.

Typical final-sale products include: - personalized engraving, - custom
furniture, - monogrammed items, - opened hygiene products, - perishable
goods, - digital downloads, - certain clearance items marked "Final
Sale."

Final-sale status does not remove the customer's ability to report a
defective, damaged, or incorrect item where the applicable defect or
fulfillment policy provides a remedy.

A customer should always be told **why** a standard return is
unavailable and which alternative process, if any, remains available.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 23. Marketplace and Seller-Fulfilled Orders

Nimbus Commerce hosts products sold directly by Nimbus Commerce and by
approved marketplace sellers.

Seller-fulfilled orders may have different: - return addresses, -
processing times, - warranty providers, - return windows, - shipping
methods, - escalation paths.

The order system must be checked before giving a seller-specific policy
answer.

If the seller is responsible for fulfillment, Nimbus Commerce support
remains the customer's initial support channel unless the order
explicitly instructs the customer to contact the seller.

Seller disputes that remain unresolved may be escalated to Marketplace
Operations.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 24. Subscription and Recurring Orders

Some eligible products can be purchased under a recurring delivery
subscription.

Customers may cancel future subscription shipments before the
cancellation cutoff displayed in their account. Cancellation of a
subscription does not automatically cancel a shipment that has already
entered fulfillment.

A customer may return a delivered subscription product according to the
normal product-category return policy unless the subscription terms
specify a different rule.

Discounts associated with subscriptions may be removed if the customer
no longer satisfies the required subscription conditions.

# 25. Refund Calculation Rules

The refund amount depends on the actual transaction rather than simply
the product's current list price.

The refund engine considers: - item selling price, - item-level
discounts, - order-level discounts, - bundle discounts, - shipping
charges, - taxes where applicable, - loyalty points, - gift-card
balance, - promotional credits, - return fees where permitted, -
partial-return allocation.

## 25.1 Example

A customer purchases: - Jacket: €80 - Shoes: €100 - Order discount:
€20 - Shipping: €5 - Total paid: €165

If the customer returns only the jacket, the €20 order-level discount
may be allocated proportionally. Therefore, the jacket refund may be
less than €80.

The exact amount should always be obtained from the order/refund system
rather than calculated manually by an agent when a system result is
available.

## 25.2 Shipping Refund

Original shipping may be refundable when the return is caused by a
Nimbus Commerce error, damaged item, incorrect item, or another
qualifying reason. Change-of-mind returns may not include original
shipping reimbursement.

# 26. Fraud, Abuse, and Repeated-Return Review

Nimbus Commerce monitors unusual patterns to protect customers and the
marketplace.

Potential review indicators include: - repeated high-value returns, -
repeated claims of non-delivery, - inconsistent serial numbers, -
frequent damaged-item claims, - payment disputes inconsistent with order
history, - suspected account takeover, - repeated use of promotional
loopholes.

A risk review does not automatically mean that a customer is fraudulent.
Support should use neutral language and should not accuse the customer
without an authorized determination.

Risk-related cases should be escalated rather than handled through
ad-hoc policy exceptions.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 27. Customer Communication Standards

Support responses should be clear, specific, and action-oriented.

A good response should normally contain: 1. the current status, 2. the
applicable policy, 3. the customer's eligibility, 4. the next action, 5.
the expected timeframe, 6. any information the customer needs to
provide.

Avoid statements such as "your refund is coming soon" when a precise
operational status is available. Prefer "Your refund was initiated on 28
August; bank posting normally requires 2--5 business days."

Agents should not promise a result that requires another team's
approval. For example, "we will replace the item" should only be used
when replacement approval is already confirmed.

# 28. Operational Definitions

**Business day:** A day on which Nimbus Commerce's relevant operational
and banking partners normally process transactions, excluding configured
weekends and holidays.

**Calendar day:** Every day, including weekends and holidays.

**Delivery date:** The date recorded by the carrier or fulfillment
system as successful delivery.

**Return request:** A customer's submitted request to return a product.
It is not the same as an approved return.

**Return authorization:** Approval to send an item back under a specific
return case.

**Inspection:** Physical or technical assessment of a returned product.

**Refund initiated:** Nimbus Commerce has sent the refund instruction to
the payment/refund system.

**Refund completed:** The relevant payment system confirms completion.

**COD:** Cash on Delivery.

**SLA:** Service Level Agreement or operational delivery target.

**Reverse pickup:** Collection of a return parcel from the customer's
address.

**Final sale:** Product status indicating that ordinary change-of-mind
returns are unavailable, subject to defect and fulfillment protections.

**Escalation:** Transfer of a case to a specialized team because
frontline support cannot complete the required action.

# 29. Frequently Asked Questions

## Can I return clothes after 25 days?

Yes, if the item is within the 30-day clothing return window and meets
the condition requirements.

## Can I return electronics after 20 days?

Normally not under the standard electronics return policy because
electronics have a 14-day return window. If the product is defective,
the warranty process may still apply.

## How long does a COD refund take?

Nimbus Commerce normally initiates an approved COD refund within 3
business days after return inspection. Bank posting may require an
additional 2--5 business days.

## Can I cancel an order after it has shipped?

A shipped order normally cannot be cancelled. The customer can use the
applicable return process after delivery.

## Can I exchange shoes for another size?

Yes, normally within 30 days, provided the shoes are in eligible
condition and the requested size is available.

## What if I received a damaged product?

Report transit damage promptly, normally within 48 hours, and provide
requested evidence. The claims team may approve replacement, refund,
repair, or another remedy.

## Can I return an opened beauty product?

Opened beauty and hygiene products are generally excluded for ordinary
returns. Defects, contamination, incorrect items, or fulfillment errors
are handled separately.

## Can I use loyalty points to pay for shipping?

Normally no. Points can generally cover up to 30% of eligible
merchandise subtotal and cannot be used for excluded fees.

## What happens after three payment failures?

The customer should stop repeatedly retrying, verify payment details,
and try another supported payment method. Risk controls may require
additional review.

## Does a late delivery automatically qualify for a refund?

No. First determine the applicable SLA, current tracking status, carrier
exception, and whether a delivery investigation is required.

## Can international customers return products?

Some products and destinations support international returns.
Restrictions and return-cost responsibility depend on the product,
destination, and reason for return.

## Who handles a privacy request?

The frontline team can initiate the approved workflow, but unresolved
privacy matters are escalated to the Privacy team.

# 30. Scenario-Based Policy Examples

## Scenario A --- Clothing return on day 28

Customer received a jacket 28 calendar days ago and wants a refund
because the size is wrong. The jacket is unworn and tags remain
attached.

**Decision:** Eligible for a standard clothing return. The customer may
request a refund or size exchange, subject to inventory and the normal
return process.

## Scenario B --- Laptop defect after 40 days

Customer reports that a laptop stopped charging 40 days after delivery.

**Decision:** The 14-day standard electronics return window has passed,
but the laptop is within its 24-month standard warranty period. Route
through warranty diagnostics.

## Scenario C --- COD refund marked processed

Customer returned shoes, received an approved refund, and says the money
has not appeared after two days.

**Decision:** Two business days may still be within the expected banking
period. Verify the refund initiation timestamp and transaction reference
before escalating.

## Scenario D --- Order shipped but customer wants cancellation

The customer requests cancellation after receiving a shipment
notification.

**Decision:** Cancellation is normally unavailable after shipment.
Explain the return process and avoid promising that the carrier can stop
delivery.

## Scenario E --- Wrong color delivered

Customer ordered black headphones and received white headphones.

**Decision:** Treat as a fulfillment error. Arrange the approved
return/pickup and replacement or refund. Customer should not bear return
shipping for a confirmed Nimbus Commerce error.

## Scenario F --- Parcel split across warehouses

Customer ordered four products and received only two. The order page
shows two active shipments.

**Decision:** Do not classify the products as missing yet. Explain that
the order is split and provide the tracking information for the
remaining shipment.

## Scenario G --- International battery shipment

Customer wants to ship a lithium-battery product to a destination where
the carrier does not accept that battery classification.

**Decision:** The shipment is restricted. Do not recommend bypassing the
restriction. Offer an eligible alternative if one exists.

## Scenario H --- Repeated damaged-item claims

A customer has submitted several high-value damage claims over a short
period.

**Decision:** Do not accuse the customer of fraud. Follow the normal
claim workflow and escalate the pattern to Risk when the established
review threshold is reached.

## Scenario I --- Size exchange unavailable

Customer wants to exchange a shirt for XL, but XL is out of stock.

**Decision:** Offer another eligible size, store credit, or refund
according to the customer's available options.

## Scenario J --- Payment failed but bank shows a charge

Customer's order shows payment failed, but the bank displays a pending
amount.

**Decision:** Check whether the amount is an authorization hold or
successful capture. Do not automatically classify it as a duplicate
charge.

## 31.2 Retrieval-Quality Requirements

The RAG system should retrieve both broad policy sections and specific
category rules.

For example, the query:

> "Can I return my laptop after 20 days?"

should retrieve the electronics return section rather than only the
general return section.

The query:

> "My COD refund was processed yesterday, when will I see it?"

should retrieve both the COD refund timeline and the distinction between
refund initiation and bank posting.

The query:

> "My order is late and has not moved for two days"

should retrieve delivery SLA, tracking-status definitions, and
carrier-investigation rules.

## 31.3 Conflicting-Rule Resolution

If retrieval returns several rules, the agent should prefer: 1.
order-specific rule, 2. product-specific rule, 3. promotion-specific
rule, 4. seller-specific rule, 5. regional/destination rule, 6. general
Nimbus policy.

The agent should not combine incompatible rules into an invented
compromise.

# 32. Policy Change and Version-Control Rules

Policy documents are versioned. When a policy changes, the new policy
must include an effective date.

Historical orders should normally be evaluated against the policy
applicable when the relevant transaction occurred, unless the policy
explicitly states otherwise.

Knowledge-base ingestion should preserve: - document version, - section
title, - effective date, - last updated date, - category, - region, -
policy priority.

When updating the vector database, old chunks should not remain active
if they have been superseded unless historical retrieval is
intentionally supported.

# Appendix B --- Agent Decision Sequence

When answering a customer-support query, the agent should reason through
the following sequence:

1.  **Identify the intent.**
2.  **Identify the order/product if an order-specific answer is
    required.**
3.  **Determine product category.**
4.  **Determine country/region when relevant.**
5.  **Determine payment method when relevant.**
6.  **Check order status.**
7.  **Retrieve the most specific applicable policy.**
8.  **Check exceptions and exclusions.**
9.  **Check whether another tool is required.**
10. **Give the customer a concise answer.**
11. **State the next action and timeframe.**
12. **Escalate if the case requires another team's authority.**

The agent must never fabricate an order status, refund status, tracking
event, inventory quantity, payment result, or approval.
